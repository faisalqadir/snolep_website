Rooted in Ladakh

Snolep is named after the snow leopard, the quiet cat of the high Himalaya. It is rarely seen, wastes no movement and is at home where little else manages.

We work from the same mountains. Signal drops here and winters are long, so we build sites that load on a weak connection and keep running when nobody is watching them.
