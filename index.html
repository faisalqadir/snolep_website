Built to thrive, not just survive

In the mountains, surviving takes grit and a thick skin. Thriving takes agility, flexibility and resilience. The snow leopard wanders the sparse terrain of the Himalaya patiently, ready at any moment to pounce.

Chasing through technical ground, it has to be fast and still change its approach and direction mid-stride. It misses, again and again, and shows up for the next one. That is how we work, from Ladakh.
